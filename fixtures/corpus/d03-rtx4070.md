# NVIDIA RTX-4070 specifications

The RTX-4070 is a mid-range Ada Lovelace GPU with 12GB of GDDR6X memory and 5888
CUDA cores. It targets 1440p gaming at a lower power budget (200W). The RTX-4070
is a popular choice for builders who want Ada features without the RTX-4090's
price or power draw. Note it is a different, smaller card than the RTX-4090.
