# NVIDIA RTX-4090 specifications

The RTX-4090 is the flagship Ada Lovelace GPU with 24GB of GDDR6X memory and
16384 CUDA cores. It targets 4K gaming and large-model inference. The RTX-4090
draws up to 450W and needs a 16-pin 12VHPWR connector. For local LLM work the
RTX-4090's 24GB lets you load mid-size quantized models without offloading.
